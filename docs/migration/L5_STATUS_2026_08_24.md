## Service Worker rollout decision

`serviceWorkerCache` remains **OFF in the migration/development runtime by design**.

Reason: the next phases will heavily change lesson forms, subject assets, AI and multilingual content. Keeping automatic production caching off during rapid content development prevents an old shell/content cache from masking source changes.

The Service Worker itself has already passed real browser/offline validation. The production flag will be enabled at the later **PWA/staging deployment gate**, followed by a final controlled rollout regression.
