# Turn 12 — Direct-semantic Strategy Layer

This layer is deterministic and source-backed.

It may expose:

- visual scene / visual asset;
- gesture;
- contrast;
- category/examples;
- analogy;
- simple Russian definition;
- audio/context.

Vietnamese and English translation fields are prohibited evidence and are never consulted.

The descriptor preserves the source field names used for every strategy so later browser/runtime QA can trace why an explanation was shown.

Turn 12 remains non-authoritative. Turn 13 is the planned learner-facing vocabulary authority switch.
