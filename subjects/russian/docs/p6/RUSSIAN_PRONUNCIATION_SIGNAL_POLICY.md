# Russian P6 Pronunciation Signal Policy

Allowed signals:
- ASR transcript similarity — LIMITED;
- recognized keyword presence — LIMITED;
- verified stress lexicon display — lexical support only;
- learner self-comparison — SELF;
- teacher/manual assessment — only when supplied by an authoritative P4 policy.

Forbidden:
- fabricated phoneme precision;
- automatic “pronunciation score” claims from transcript similarity;
- ASR threshold directly setting mastery;
- inferred stress diagnosis when no capable signal exists.

Feedback must say what the signal can actually support. Unknown stress remains visibly unknown rather than guessed.
