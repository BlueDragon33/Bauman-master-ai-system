# Russian P7 Linguistic Authority Constitution

## Mission
P7 owns linguistic truth validation without becoming a second content owner. Existing canonical datasets remain owned by P3. P7 attaches validation/provenance/confidence and rejects unsupported promotion to linguistic authority.

## Linguistic responsibilities
P7 validates:
- Russian orthography and naturalness;
- stress;
- morphology and part of speech;
- case/preposition/verb government;
- aspect pairing;
- collocation;
- register;
- academic/technical terminology;
- provenance;
- generated-content promotion.

## Authority rule
**NO SOURCE · NO VERIFIED CLAIM.**

A field may be treated as linguistically canonical only when its provenance record contains an inspectable source reference and review evidence appropriate to the claim. Latin transliteration, browser TTS, ASR similarity, AI output and frequency alone are not linguistic authority.

## Source precedence
1. normative/official linguistic or terminological authority;
2. authoritative institutional/domain glossary or technical standard;
3. curated academic dictionary/corpus/textbook with identifiable edition/source;
4. project-local imported source with provenance but no independent validation;
5. generated/inferred content.

Levels 4–5 cannot be promoted to `VERIFIED` without additional evidence.

## Confidence states
- `VERIFIED` — source evidence + reviewer evidence; canonical claim allowed.
- `SOURCE_ASSERTED` — source states the claim but P7 review is incomplete.
- `PARTIAL` — some fields supported; unsupported fields remain explicitly unknown.
- `UNVERIFIED` — usable as legacy/source content but not linguistic authority.
- `REJECTED` — contradicted, malformed or unsafe to teach as truth.

## Stress
- explicit acute-marked Cyrillic from a reviewed source may be authoritative;
- `ё` is an orthographic cue, not a blanket proof for every phrase;
- Latin transliteration must never be used to infer Russian stress;
- TTS playback is a listening aid, not stress provenance.

## Morphology and government
POS, forms, declension/conjugation, government and aspect pairs require explicit source-backed fields. Empty fields are preferable to guessed morphology.

## Register and naturalness
Naturalness/register labels require context: situation, interlocutor, academic/technical domain and source evidence. A grammatically possible sentence is not automatically natural.

## Technical terminology
Technical terms must retain domain and source provenance. P8 may deepen technical content but consumes this P7 validation policy rather than inventing a second authority system.

## Generated content
Generated candidates start `UNVERIFIED`. They may be used for temporary practice only where P10 allows it, but cannot overwrite canonical content or become `VERIFIED` automatically.

## Foundation boundaries
P7 does not own mastery, SRS, planner, audio, recording, recognition or UI architecture.
