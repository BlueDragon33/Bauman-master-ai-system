# Russian P10 AI Generation Policy

Temporary generation is allowed for:
- hints;
- retrieval prompts;
- contrastive examples;
- reformulation practice;
- scenario variations;
- comprehension questions;
- draft outlines;
- research-question prompts.

Every generated item is `EPHEMERAL_UNTIL_REVIEWED`.
It may reference canonical IDs but may not overwrite them.

Promotion to canonical content requires P12 authoring/review/provenance validation. Generated citations or normative claims are forbidden unless backed by an approved source reference.
