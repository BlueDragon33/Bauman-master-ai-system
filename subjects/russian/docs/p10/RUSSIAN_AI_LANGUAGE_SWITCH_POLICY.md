# Russian P10 Language Switching Policy

Supported modes: Vietnamese, Russian, English, Vietnamese-Russian, English-Russian, Vietnamese-English.

Default teaching explanation may use Vietnamese for clarity while Russian remains the target language.

Rules:
- preserve Russian technical terms when they are the learning target;
- do not translate away recognition/production objectives;
- allow concise Vietnamese clarification for grammar/strategy;
- allow English as an auxiliary technical bridge where useful;
- generated Russian examples remain non-canonical unless validated under P7/P12.
