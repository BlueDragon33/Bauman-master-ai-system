# Russian P11 Scenario Progression Policy

Progression is experiential, not mastery authority.

Required families: real-life, administration, classroom, lab, seminar, research and defense.

Every scenario must include explicit roles, a learner goal, branching, at least one unexpected turn, at least one repair path, and a practice-only completion state.

Difficulty may rise through faster or denser turns, incomplete information, clarification demands, role pressure, technical specificity, or defense/research follow-up. Difficulty must never be represented by silently changing official scores, SRS due dates or mastery.
