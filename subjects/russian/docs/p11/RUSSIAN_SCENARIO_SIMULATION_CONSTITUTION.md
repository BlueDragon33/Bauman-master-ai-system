# Russian P11 Scenario · Simulation · Dialogue Experience Constitution

## Mission
Turn P2–P10 curriculum capabilities into integrated real-world practice while reusing the P6 dialogue/speech runtime.

## Canonical ownership
- P6 owns audio, recorder, recognition adapter and dialogue execution.
- P4 owns official assessment/mastery.
- P5 owns planner/SRS priority.
- P7 owns linguistic truth.
- P8/P9 own academic, technical and research language contracts.
- P10 AI may coach inside a scenario but remains non-authoritative.
- P11 owns scenario composition, roles, branching, unexpected turns and practice progression only.

## Hard rules
1. No second recorder, SpeechRecognition wrapper, audio engine or dialogue state engine.
2. Every scenario references existing speaking contexts through `speaking-link-index.json`.
3. Scenario completion is practice evidence only and cannot auto-master, auto-pass or auto-unlock.
4. Every integrated scenario must expose at least one repair path.
5. Branches represent communication choices or unexpected turns, not hidden mastery mutation.
6. Mic/ASR failure falls back to the P6 typed/self-comparison/manual path.
7. Generated AI turns are temporary coaching unless P12 promotes reviewed content.
8. Scenario state is transient/recoverable and must not corrupt canonical learner state.

## Exit
Representative real-life, administration, classroom, lab, seminar, research and defense contexts can be practiced through one scenario contract without duplicating P6 engines.
