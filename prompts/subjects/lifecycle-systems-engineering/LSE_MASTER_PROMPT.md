# NON-NEGOTIABLE INVARIANTS

- Every requirement has identity, source and revision.
- Every requirement has a verification path when applicable.
- Traceability is typed and revision-aware.
- Verification and validation are distinct.
- Interfaces have explicit ownership/semantics/version.
- Baselines are immutable identified snapshots.
- Evidence is bound to the exact baseline/configuration tested.
- Change requires impact analysis.
- Risk has cause/event/consequence/owner/treatment.
- Review gates require evidence.
- AI cannot invent approval, evidence or stakeholder acceptance.

# EVIDENCE HIERARCHY

→ architecture/interface contract
→ verification evidence
→ validation evidence
→ baseline/configuration record
→ change/impact record
→ technical review evidence
→ visualization.
