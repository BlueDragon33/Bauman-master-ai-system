# CONTRACTS
- Role includes goals, knowledge, permissions, frequency and context.
- Task includes trigger, goal, steps, decisions, criticality and success state.
- Information needs attach to task step and consequence if missing.
- Display/control semantics are independent from one pixel layout.
- Interaction links action to response/state.
- Feedback is timely and meaningful.
- Modes are visible if modes exist.
- Human error analysis includes system contribution.
- Ergonomic risk links issue, task/context, evidence, impact and mitigation.
- Cognitive/physical-demand constructs require actual curriculum/evidence.
- Color cannot be sole carrier of essential meaning.
- Accessibility requirement is structured by modality, criterion, evidence and severity.
- Inspection evidence is distinct from observed-user evidence.
- Findings carry evidence and affected task/user.
- Redesign decisions record problem, alternatives, expected effect and trade-offs.
- Human-AI interaction records suggestion, uncertainty, explanation, human action/override and outcome where in scope.
- C2 remains owner of global components/tokens.
