# MATH AUTHORING SCHEMA CONTRACT

Owner: MATH05 · Status: ACTIVE

Normal maintenance is no-code and entity-based.

Authorable entities:
- concept/definition/notation/formula/theorem;
- worked problem/problem template/solution path;
- misconception/remediation;
- assessment item;
- graph/visualization configuration;
- application/project;
- provenance/source metadata.

A problem authoring record MUST include competency, prerequisites, prompt, domain constraints, response type, equivalence/solution policy, hints, misconception mapping and validation fixtures.

Parametric variants additionally require bounded ranges, constraints, deterministic seed, degeneracy checks, expected-answer derivation and batch validation.

Generated answer keys cannot become ACTIVE without validation/review.
