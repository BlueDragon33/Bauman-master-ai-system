# MATH03 · PROOF & REASONING POLICY

Status: ACTIVE

Proof/reasoning evidence is separate from the final answer.

A proof contract may declare hypotheses, claims/subgoals, accepted proof methods, required justification criteria and counterexample handling. Criteria are explicit and may include:
- states hypotheses/domain;
- establishes each dependency;
- uses a valid inference;
- reaches the requested conclusion;
- handles exceptional cases;
- communicates rigor appropriate to the course.

## Authority
No general formal proof verifier exists in current Math runtime. Therefore:
- free-form proof submissions default to `INDETERMINATE / REQUIRES_VALIDATED_REVIEW`;
- AI critique may suggest weaknesses but cannot award official proof score;
- criterion-based partial credit may be recorded only from a validated human/formal-provider result;
- a correct final statement does not prove valid reasoning.

Alternative valid proof methods are allowed when they satisfy the same problem criteria.
