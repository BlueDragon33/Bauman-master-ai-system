# NON-NEGOTIABLE INVARIANTS

- Time order is first-class.
- Forecast origin and horizon are explicit.
- Future observations cannot leak into training/past features.
- Temporal split/backtest is part of model truth.
- Random split is not default for time series.
- Scaling/imputation/feature engineering must respect temporal fitting boundaries.
- Stationarity is model/context dependent, not a ritual checkbox.
- ACF/PACF are diagnostic evidence, not automatic oracle.
- Baseline forecast is required where meaningful.
- Training-fit quality is not forecast quality.
- Prediction uncertainty must be contextualized.
- Neural forecasting reuses NN owner and temporal evaluation remains TS-owned.
- AI cannot invent future observations, forecast metrics or hidden test results.
