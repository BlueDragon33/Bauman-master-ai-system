# NON-NEGOTIABLE INVARIANTS

- Problem precedes solution confidence.
- Customer segment is explicit.
- Market/customer claims require evidence.
- Business-model assumptions are explicit.
- Experiments test assumptions and have decision criteria.
- Revenue, profit, margin and cash flow are not synonyms.
- Market size is estimate, not guaranteed sales.
- Traction has provenance.
- Financial scenarios declare assumptions.
- AI cannot fabricate market evidence, interviews, customers, revenue, funding or traction.
- Entrepreneurship does not override project/technical truth owned by adjacent subjects.
