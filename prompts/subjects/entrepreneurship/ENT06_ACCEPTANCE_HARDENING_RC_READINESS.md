# ENT06 — ACCEPTANCE · HARDENING · LEGACY CLOSURE · RC READINESS

Mode:

`EVIDENCE-REGRESSION · ECONOMICS-RED-TEAMED · CLAIM-SKEPTICAL · NO-KNOWN-BLOCKER`

# MISSION

Prove Entrepreneurship is evidence-grounded, assumption-transparent, economically coherent, accessible and exact-RC ready.

# TEST MATRIX

## Problem / Customer
- solution-first case;
- vague problem;
- segment too broad;
- buyer/user confusion;
- customer evidence missing.

## Evidence
- fabricated interview;
- unattributed market claim;
- stale market evidence;
- opinion presented as observation;
- survey intent overclaim.

## Competition / Alternatives
- “no competition” with no evidence;
- alternative ignored;
- competitor comparison with mismatched criteria.

## Value / Business Model
- value proposition not tied to customer need;
- key assumption hidden;
- internally inconsistent business model.

## Experiment
- no hypothesis;
- no metric;
- no decision criterion;
- result does not answer assumption;
- retrospective success criterion.

## Market Sizing
Only if in scope:
- TAM/SAM/SOM confusion;
- wrong units;
- duplicate population;
- unsupported conversion;
- obtainable revenue overclaim.

## Pricing / Revenue
Only if in scope:
- price with no evidence;
- wrong price unit;
- revenue formula mismatch;
- growth assumption unsupported.

## Costs / Unit Economics
Only if in scope:
- hidden cost;
- inconsistent period/unit;
- margin error;
- CAC/LTV misuse;
- break-even mismatch;
- contribution/profit confusion.

## Financial Scenario
Only if in scope:
- revenue = cash flow;
- profit = cash;
- scenario presented as certainty;
- impossible assumption stack.

## Traction
- projected metric presented as observed;
- vanity metric presented as strong traction without context;
- duplicated/invalid evidence.

## Pitch / Claims
- unsupported market claim;
- unsupported competitive claim;
- unsupported traction;
- unsupported financial claim.

## AI
AI must not:
- fabricate customer evidence;
- fabricate market size/source;
- fabricate revenue/traction;
- fabricate funding;
- turn estimates into facts;
- write official mastery.
