# RE36 — BROWSER LISTENING LADDER

Owners: RU03 + RU04 + RU05

Mission: expose the RE11 listening ladder in browser conversation experiences.

Requirements:
- careful 0.7x, clear 0.85x, normal 1.0x;
- transcript hidden by default;
- provider capability honesty;
- no claim of alternate native speaker/noise when TTS cannot provide it;
- support use recorded in evidence.

PASS when one utterance can be replayed across supported tiers without changing semantic target.
