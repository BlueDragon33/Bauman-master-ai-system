# RE12 — PRONUNCIATION LAB

Canonical owners: RU03 + RU04 + RU05

Mission: train Russian sound production honestly, with separate evidence for ASR transcript, acoustic signal, stress/rhythm and learner self-comparison.

## Priorities
- hard/soft consonants;
- Ж/Ш/Щ/Ч/Ц;
- Р;
- Ы;
- stressed/unstressed vowels;
- final devoicing;
- assimilation;
- word stress;
- phrase stress;
- rhythm;
- intonation;
- connected speech.

## Evidence honesty
Plain browser ASR NEVER becomes phoneme/stress mastery.
Every pronunciation signal declares:
- provider;
- capability;
- confidence meaning;
- known limitations.

## Practice progression
LISTEN → DISCRIMINATE → IMITATE → RECORD → COMPARE → RETRY → SHADOW → PRODUCE IN CONTEXT.

## Canonical truth
Stress, pronunciation facts and accepted variants must resolve through RU03 authority.

## Exit
PASS when pronunciation practice can combine canonical targets with honest provider signals while explicitly blocking unsupported phoneme/mastery claims.
