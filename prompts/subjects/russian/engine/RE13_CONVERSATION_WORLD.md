# RE13 — CONVERSATION WORLD

Canonical owners: RU05 + RU07 + RU03 + RU04

Mission: make conversation a stateful world interaction rather than an empty chatbot.

## State
- setting;
- participants/roles;
- relationship/register;
- shared facts;
- hidden facts;
- learner goal;
- current turn;
- valid actions;
- repair routes;
- consequences;
- completion.

## Success
Goal-based, not exact-string matching.
Multiple natural replies may satisfy one communicative function.

## Repair
Repair is a first-class skill:
repeat, slower, clarify, confirm, reformulate, ask meaning, check understanding.

## AI boundary
AI may vary wording and act as partner.
It may not alter scenario truth, grant mastery, fabricate canonical Russian, or make offline core scenarios unusable.

## Exit
PASS when deterministic and optional-AI paths preserve the same world truth and evidence contract, including at least one misunderstanding-repair-success journey.
