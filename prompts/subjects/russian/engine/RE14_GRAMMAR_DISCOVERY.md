# RE14 — GRAMMAR DISCOVERY

Canonical owners: RU02 + RU03 + RU04

Mission: teach Russian grammar through exposure, contrast and prediction before formal explanation when pedagogically appropriate.

Default order:
EXPOSURE → PATTERN → CONTRAST → EXPECTATION → DISCOVERY → EXPLANATION → TRANSFER.

## Rules
Do not hide grammar permanently.
Do not lead with large declension/conjugation tables by default.
Use meaningful chunks and scenes first.
Formal terminology appears when it helps abstraction and university-level learning.

## Discovery units
May include:
- agreement;
- case function;
- aspect;
- motion;
- tense;
- word order;
- negation;
- question patterns;
- government;
- register.

## Evidence
Distinguish recognition, prediction, controlled production and spontaneous production.

## Exit
PASS when one grammar concept is learned through repeated contextual contrast, then explicitly named/explained, then transferred to unseen content.
