# RU06 — ACADEMIC · TECHNICAL · READING · WRITING · НИР · ВКР PRODUCTION
## Russian for university, engineering, research and defense

Former source responsibility: P8 + P9.

---

# 0. MISSION

Transform validated Russian into productive academic/technical performance.

The learner should progress from understanding university input to producing explanations, reports, research writing, presentations and defense answers.

---

# 1. CONSTITUTION ROUTING

Load:

- C4: outcomes, competency/evidence, transfer, projects/artifacts, research learning;
- C1: content/resource/provenance structures;
- C2: reading/writing/resource/AI contextual UX as needed;
- C3: content/assessment/browser/regression QA.

Consume RU03 truth and RU04 evidence policy.

---

→ listening/reading

→ transfer.

- messages/forms/notices;
- university email/instruction;
- article/textbook;
- technical documentation;
- abstract;
- paper fragment;
- research source.

locate
→ infer
→ classify
→ extract
→ outline
→ compare
→ summarize
→ explain
→ synthesize.

# 5. NOTE-TAKING

- lecture structure;
- definitions;
- examples;
- cause/result;
- enumeration;

- abbreviations;
- reconstruction after listening/reading.

# 6. WRITING PROGRESSION

sentence/message/form/email
→ paragraph
→ summary
→ technical explanation
→ lab note/report
→ abstract
→ НИР section
→ ВКР section.

Handwriting ability is not equivalent to academic written production.

---

# 7. SOURCE-BASED WRITING

Maintain traceability:

source
→ extracted claim
→ learner note
→ paraphrase/synthesis
→ draft section.

Distinguish:

- source fact;
- interpretation;
- learner claim;
- generated suggestion.

Do not fabricate sources/citations.

# 12. DOCUMENT / SOURCE LIFECYCLE

For serious source-based work preserve:

- source identity/version;
- draft identity;
- submission snapshot;
- feedback provenance;
- revision intent;
- citation/figure/table provenance;
- correction/retraction effects.

Do not silently rewrite an official submission.
