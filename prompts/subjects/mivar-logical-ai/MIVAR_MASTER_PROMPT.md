# NON-NEGOTIABLE INVARIANTS

- Facts, rules, variables and domains are explicit.
- Every derived fact has provenance.
- Every inference step names the rule and bindings used.
- Hidden facts are forbidden.
- Circular inference has termination strategy.
- Contradictions are surfaced, not silently overwritten.
- Rule priority/conflict strategy is explicit if used.
- Equivalent derivations may coexist.
- Final answer without valid trace is insufficient evidence.
- LLM-generated prose cannot replace deterministic proof/inference.
- AI cannot fabricate rules, facts or derivation history.
- Knowledge-base revision identity is explicit.
