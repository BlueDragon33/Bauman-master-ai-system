# NON-NEGOTIABLE INVARIANTS

- Research problem and question are explicit.
- Claims are traceable to evidence.
- Evidence has provenance.
- Source metadata is not fabricated.
- Hypothesis and outcome variables are explicit where applicable.
- Exploratory and confirmatory reasoning are not silently conflated.
- Correlation is not causation.
- Statistical significance is not practical importance.
- Null/non-significant result is not automatically evidence of no effect.
- Negative results are valid scientific outcomes.
- Reproducibility metadata is first-class.
- AI cannot invent sources, data, results, consent or peer-review outcomes.
- Research ethics and attribution are not optional decoration.

# EVIDENCE HIERARCHY

primary source / raw evidence
→ validated derived evidence
→ reproducible analysis
→ secondary synthesis
→ interpretation
→ visualization
→ screenshot.

Hierarchy can vary by research design; provenance remains mandatory.
