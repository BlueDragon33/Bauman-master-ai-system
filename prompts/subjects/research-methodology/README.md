# 10. STEP 4 — RSW04

Open:

`RSW04_EVIDENCE_LITERATURE_EXPERIMENT_AI.md`.

Goal:

**provide evidence, literature, experiment and AI capabilities without allowing automation to become the research authority.**

Potential capabilities:

- literature/source search adapters;
- citation manager;
- DOI/metadata validation;
- source annotation;
- claim-evidence graph;
- research notebook/log;
- experiment protocol builder;
- reproducibility checker;
- dataset/artifact provenance;
- statistical-analysis adapters;
- figure/table validation;
- plagiarism/attribution support;
- peer-review workspace;
- AI Research Tutor.

Rules:

- AI-generated citation ≠ valid citation;
- abstract ≠ full evidence;
- source popularity ≠ source quality;
- p-value ≠ practical importance;
- correlation ≠ causation;
- absence of significance ≠ proof of no effect;
- reproducibility ≠ replicability;
- AI cannot invent sources, DOIs, data, results or participant consent.
